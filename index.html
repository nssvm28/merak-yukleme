Merak Yukleme

Merak Yukleme is a private, single-user publishing tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Merak Makinesi (Turkish educational videos about geography and world facts). It uploads the channel's own finished videos, with their title, description, tags and thumbnail, through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
